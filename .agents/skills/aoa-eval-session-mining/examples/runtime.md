# Example

## Scenario

The user asks to mine sessions for missed eval triggers after research and repo
owner surfaces are mapped.

## Why this skill fits

- the target is session-derived eval pressure
- `.aoa` refs can expose real missed trigger cases
- raw evidence must remain candidate-only

## Expected inputs

- trigger classes from prior research
- `.aoa` search provider status and freshness
- target repositories and owner boundaries
- raw and segment refs for candidate evidence

## Expected outputs

- missed-trigger examples with session id, segment, event, raw ref, and freshness
- candidate owner route and rejected nearest wrong owner
- local need, design, apply, or stop handoff

## Boundary notes

- session mining is not `.aoa` hook repair
- raw session evidence is not reviewed proof
- mining starts after owner boundaries are known

## Verification notes

- verify index freshness and provider
- verify raw and segment refs for each candidate
- verify no proof promotion occurs
