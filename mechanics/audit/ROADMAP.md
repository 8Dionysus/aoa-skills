# Audit Roadmap

## Current Contour

Audit owns public-surface, evaluation, governance backlog, review-record, and
proof-readout pressure for the skill layer. It keeps generated evidence useful
without turning generated files into authority.

Active surfaces include `mechanics/audit/docs/PUBLIC_SURFACE.md`,
`generated/public_surface.*`, `generated/governance_backlog.*`,
`generated/governance_backlog.md`, `generated/skill_evaluation_matrix.*`,
`generated/skill_evaluation_matrix.md`, `generated/overlay_readiness.md`,
review records under `docs/reviews/`, and trigger-boundary or autonomy fixtures
under `tests/fixtures/`.

## Next Work

- Keep `v0.3.x` public-core hardening visible as residual audit work: validator
  parity, status-aware floors, pinned technique traceability, drift detection,
  and autonomy checks.
- Keep the `v0.4` evaluation harness current through comparative boundary
  fixtures, fixture integrity checks, and richer end-to-end evidence for
  default-reference decisions.
- Keep governance backlog and candidate review records here when they explain
  evidence quality rather than runtime behavior.
- Keep public-surface counts derived from generated views instead of copying
  counts into source docs.
- Tighten generated/public readout references after full release checks expose
  drift.

## When Time Comes

- Split proof-facing follow-up to `aoa-evals` when audit evidence becomes a
  reusable evaluation template rather than repo-local readout hygiene.
- Add a package-local audit validator only after the same public-surface or
  review-record mistake repeats.
- Add maintenance evidence refresh rules when canonical bundles need repeated
  proof review to stay default references.

## Out Of Scope

- Runtime activation, release packaging, and downstream integration authority.
- Promotion by gate pass alone; default-reference rationale belongs in
  `method-growth` and evidence quality belongs here.
- Duplicating generated reports as hand-written source truth.
