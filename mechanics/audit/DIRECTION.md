# Audit Direction

Audit stays a reader and routing package.

The active route is:

```text
skill evidence or public claim pressure
  -> local audit contract or evidence guide
  -> generated readout or review record
  -> bounded fix, owner route, or proof route
```

## Current contour

- `docs/AUDIT_CONTRACT.md` is the repo-local audit contract.
- `docs/EVALUATION_PATH.md` explains behavior evidence.
- `docs/PUBLIC_SURFACE.md` explains derived public and governance readouts.
- Trigger and description activation checks remain local audit evidence.
- Review records remain in `docs/reviews/` as decision evidence.

## Boundary

Audit can say what evidence exists and which checks constrain it. It cannot
turn evidence into proof doctrine or public release support by itself.
