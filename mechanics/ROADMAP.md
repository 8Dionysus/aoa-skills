# Mechanics Roadmap

Current work is limited to:

- keep Agon candidates explicitly requested and non-promoted;
- make Questbook independent of retired skill identities and use it only for
  durable unresolved obligations;
- keep portable export and pack handoff reproducible across directory and ZIP
  transport;
- remove a mechanic when its contract is absorbed by capability, skill, or a
  stronger owner.

Capability evolution, manual comparisons, lifecycle status, and task-local DAG
work route through `capabilities/`, current bundles, and session evidence rather
than new mechanics packages.
