# Agon Direction

Agon in `aoa-skills` exists to keep lawful-move workflow pressure bounded at
the skill layer.

The current active route is requested-only:

- preserve candidate inputs under `config/`
- generate compact candidate companions under `generated/`
- keep recurrence observation routes explicit and non-mutating
- validate candidate surfaces without promoting them
- send real skill creation through the normal `skills/` review path

## Current posture

The package is a bridge, not a law source.

Agon may produce pressure for bounded workflows. It may not create arena law,
proof verdicts, scars, routing authority, runtime activation, or ToS canon.

## Near pressure

- keep old flat Agon docs links fully moved into this package
- keep recurrence manifests pointed at package-local active parts
- keep generated candidate routes requested-only
- decide later whether any candidate has enough reviewed practice evidence to
  become a real skill bundle

## Stop line

No candidate becomes a skill because it has an Agon name. Skill promotion needs
normal bundle review, technique traceability where relevant, trigger boundaries,
risks, and verification.
