# Experience Direction

Experience stays owner-local and consent-bound.

The active route is:

```text
Experience operation pressure
  -> owner-local skill contract
  -> explicit review, hold, receipt, rollback, or handoff
  -> stronger owner route when release, runtime, ToS, or policy truth is needed
```

## Current contour

- Governance, stay, hold, installation, receipt, office, service, rollback, and
  runtime-governance skill notes live under package-local `docs/`.
- These docs are active contract surfaces, not root-level status docs.
- They may consume upstream Experience law, but they do not author it.

## Boundary

Experience skill surfaces may prepare and constrain local workflows. They do
not approve release, activate runtime, or write ToS meaning.
