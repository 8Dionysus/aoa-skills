# Experience Roadmap

## Current Contour

Experience owns workflow-facing contract notes and examples that show how
skills are invoked, governed, held, patched, or handed off. It does not land
owner requests by describing them.

Active pressure includes office/service handoff examples, governance runtime
invocation, receipt generation patches, policy holds, rollback drills, and the
`ORQ-EXPERIENCE-SKILLS-001` owner-request posture.

## Next Work

- Decide whether repeated contract notes should become canonical skill bundles,
  playbooks, or remain examples.
- Add schema-backed receipts only after an active contract needs executable
  validation.
- Keep `ORQ-EXPERIENCE-SKILLS-001` below landed status until reviewed owner
  acceptance exists.
- Keep usage-oriented public entrypoints aligned with audit readouts and
  release-support packaging notes without duplicating either.

## When Time Comes

- Promote an experience pattern when it repeats across two workflow examples or
  becomes a stable invocation shape for a canonical skill.
- Route recurring scenario composition to `aoa-playbooks` rather than expanding
  this package into scenario canon.
- Route proof templates to `aoa-evals` once the contract needs portable
  evaluation rather than repo-local example clarity.

## Out Of Scope

- Runtime implementation, release packaging, and owner acceptance.
- In-app or narrative explanation of skill meaning that should stay in
  `skills/**/SKILL.md`.
