This lane preserves Growth-cycle-owned harvest notes from real sessions so
skill, mechanic, proof, memory, quest, playbook, or owner follow-through work
can be grounded in public-safe evidence without turning the note itself into
promotion authority.

`mechanics/growth-cycle/AGENTS.md`, this lane `README.md`, and any linked

active source truth until the owning skill, mechanic, config, review, decision,
or sibling-owner surface is updated.
