# Method-Growth Direction

This package started with candidate lineage after reviewed donor harvest and
now carries a second bounded method-growth slice: explicit adoption lifecycle
posture for skill-layer uptake.

The active route is:

```text
checkpoint or donor carry
  -> reviewed donor harvest
  -> candidate_ref
  -> reviewed owner-status landing
  -> governed followthrough decision
  -> owner, seed, proof, memory, stats, playbook, defer, merge, or drop route

shared pattern or skill uptake pressure
  -> adoption boundary
  -> owner decision or receipt
  -> regression, retention, retirement, pattern handoff, hold, or owner route

repeated skill use pressure
  -> promotion pressure readout
  -> blocker repair, status review, canonical review, overlay adoption review, or watch
```

## Current contour

- `aoa-skills` may mint reviewed `candidate_ref` through the donor-harvest
  route.
- `aoa-skills` may preserve the first owner-status landing for a skill-layer
  candidate.
- `aoa-skills` may record the next bounded followthrough verdict.
- Later session-growth kernel examples may carry existing `candidate_ref`, but
  they do not mint seed or object truth.
- `aoa-skills` may preserve adoption compatibility, owner-decision,
  regression, retention, retirement, and pattern-handoff posture without
  approving adoption for another owner.
- `aoa-skills` may derive lived-use promotion pressure without changing skill
  status or canonical authority.

## Boundaries

- Checkpoint carry remains provisional until reviewed donor harvest.
- Owner-status landing is weaker than final owner object truth.
- Followthrough verdicts are reviewed decisions, not runners or schedules.
- Adoption is a second sovereign act after shared pattern approval.
- Owner-local consent, rollback, shadow proof, and retention evidence stay
  mandatory before durable behavior changes.
- Generated readouts and runtime notes are evidence companions, not authority.
- Promotion pressure is a review queue signal, not automatic promotion.

## Current hold

Do not move governance, session-growth maturity, questbook, checkpoint, or
release-support surfaces into this package during this slice. Governance v0.8
and operator-facing installation or consent posture remain experience or
release-support pressure until their own active route is clear.
